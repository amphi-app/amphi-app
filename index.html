How do I get the file?
  1. Log into ZEUS with your school Office 365 account.
  2. In the left sidebar, open Groupes and go down to your own group: EPITA → CLASSES PREPARATOIRES (years 1–2) or CYCLE INGENIEUR (year 3) → your campus → your year → tick your group. Your group is in your EPITA welcome email.
  3. Click Générer un ICS and download the file. iPhone: open the link, tap download, and the file goes to Files → Downloads. Computer: it downloads straight away.
  4. Come back here and choose that file.

ZEUS changes during the semester, so download a fresh file every week or two; Amphi shows how old your copy is. Keep your ZEUS link private: anyone who has it can see timetables.

Have a ZEUS link? ZEUS doesn't let Amphi read links directly yet. Once it does, paste your link here and Amphi will keep itself up to date.

Your link is saved — the app refreshes from it every time you open it.
