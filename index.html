ZEUS Timetable

Your EPITA schedule, in English. Set it up once, then it's one tap.

Your link is saved — the app refreshes from it every time you open it.

How do I get my link?
  1. Log into ZEUS with your school Office 365 account.
  2. In the left sidebar, open Groupes and go down to your own group: EPITA → CLASSES PREPARATOIRES (years 1–2) or CYCLE INGENIEUR (year 3) → your campus → your year → tick your group. Your group is in your EPITA welcome email.
  3. Click Générer un ICS, copy the link it gives you, and paste it above.

You only do this once. Keep the link private: anyone who has it can see your timetable.
